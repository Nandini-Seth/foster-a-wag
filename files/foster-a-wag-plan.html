Implementation Plan · 2026

Foster A Wag

A web platform connecting rescue operations with foster families to find loving temporary homes for animals in need.

01 — Overview

What are we building?

Foster A Wag is a two-sided platform where rescue organizations post animals needing temporary homes, and fosters discover and apply to care for them.

🏡
Foster
Individuals and families who register their home and availability to temporarily care for rescue animals.
🏥
Rescue Organization
Registered organizations that post animals needing foster placement and manage incoming applications.
🔑
Admin
Platform moderators who verify organizations, manage disputes, and ensure platform integrity.

03 — Features

Feature breakdown

All requirements grouped by the three platform categories. Click each tab to explore.

Self-Registration as a Foster
A dedicated sign-up flow at /register/foster where individuals create an account and are assigned the FOSTER role. Email verification is sent on completion.
Form Fields
Full Name Email Password Email Verification
Store Contact Information
After signup, fosters complete their profile with contact details stored in the foster_profiles table. Contact info is never exposed publicly — it's shared only after a match is initiated.
Form Fields
Phone Number City Province / State Postal Code
Store Home Setup
Fosters describe their living situation to help rescues assess suitability for specific pets. Stored as a JSONB field for flexibility. Required to unlock browsing pet listings.
Form Fields
Dwelling Type Fenced Backyard No. of Adults No. of Children Other Pets Outdoor Space
Store Availability Date
Fosters set a start date and optional end date for their availability window. This data surfaces in the rescue-facing foster browse view and is used to filter relevant matches.
Form Fields
Available From (date picker) Available Until (optional)
Store Pet Preferences
Fosters indicate which types of animals they're open to fostering, as well as sub-preferences for size, age group, and willingness to take on special needs pets.
Form Fields
Species (multi-select) Preferred Size Age Range Special Needs OK?
Setup Availability Reminders
Fosters set how often they want to be reminded to confirm or update their availability. A background cron job queries fosters whose reminder date is due and sends a prompt email via Resend/SendGrid, then schedules the next reminder.
Options
Weekly Monthly Custom Interval Cron Job: node-cron / Vercel Cron
Rescue Organization Registration
A dedicated sign-up flow at /register/rescue creates a RESCUE-role user and a linked rescue_profiles record. Organizations can optionally upload a logo for their profile page.
Form Fields
Org Name Email Phone City / Province Website (optional) Logo Upload
Post Details of Pet for Fostering
A multi-step form at /dashboard/rescue/pets/new walks rescue staff through creating a complete pet profile. The pet record is linked to the rescue and immediately visible on the public browse page upon completion.
Multi-Step Form Sections
Basic Info Health Status Personality & Needs Availability Photos Location Contact Info
Upload Pet Photos
Drag-and-drop photo uploader (react-dropzone) that sends images to Cloudinary or S3. Up to 10 photos per pet. The first photo is set as the primary image for listing cards. Images are stored in a pet_photos table with URLs and an is_primary flag.
Implementation
react-dropzone Cloudinary / S3 Up to 10 images Primary photo flag
Detailed Pet Info (Age, Breed, Training, Location)
Structured fields ensure every listing is rich and searchable. Location is stored at city-level for privacy. All fields populate the searchable filter system on the public browse page.
Key Fields
Age Breed House Trained? Spay / Neuter Status Vaccinated? Good w/ Kids / Dogs / Cats City (no street address)
Post Availability Date
Each pet listing includes a "available from" date. An optional "urgent by" field can flag animals with an urgent placement deadline, surfacing them prominently in search results.
Fields
Available From (date) Urgent By (optional)
Rescue Contact Info on Listing
Each pet listing displays the rescue organization's name, city, and a "Contact Rescue" button. Contact details are surfaced after a match or request is initiated, protecting inbox integrity.
Displayed On Listing
Org Name City / Region Logo Contact Button
Foster Selects a Pet & Sends Request
On a pet's detail page, a logged-in foster clicks "I'm Interested in Fostering [Name]." The system checks that the foster's profile is complete before allowing the request. A foster_requests record is created with PENDING status and the rescue receives an email notification.
Flow
Pet Detail Page CTA Profile Completeness Check foster_requests record Email to Rescue
Rescue Reaches Out to a Foster
Rescues can browse a filtered list of foster profiles at /fosters (filtering by availability, location, home type, and pet preferences). They can click "Reach Out" to send a message to a specific foster about a specific pet. Creates a rescue_outreach record and notifies the foster by email.
Flow
Foster Browse + Filters Reach Out Button rescue_outreach record Email to Foster
Foster Submits Application Form
After initial interest is expressed (either direction), the foster completes a formal application at /apply/[pet_id]. The rescue can then Accept, Decline, or Request More Info. Status updates trigger email notifications to the foster at each stage.
Application Fields
Motivation / Why This Pet Daily Schedule Vet Reference Personal Reference Terms Agreement Digital Signature

06 — Timeline

Implementation phases

A 12-week rollout broken into 5 focused phases, from scaffolding to launch.

1
Foundation
Weeks 1–3
  • Project scaffolding: Next.js, Tailwind, Prisma, PostgreSQL
  • Auth system: registration, login, email verification, role assignment
  • Foster registration form and profile storage
  • Rescue registration form and profile storage
  • Navigation, layout, dashboard shells
2
Core Features
Weeks 4–6
  • Multi-step pet posting form with photo upload (Cloudinary/S3)
  • Pet browse page with search and filters
  • Pet detail page
  • Foster profile browse page (rescue-facing)
  • Foster profile detail page
3
Matching & Applications
Weeks 7–9
  • Foster "I'm Interested" request flow with profile completeness gate
  • Rescue "Reach Out to Foster" flow
  • Email notifications for all match actions (Resend/SendGrid)
  • Formal application form (foster → rescue)
  • Application status tracking on both dashboards
4
Reminders & Polish
Weeks 10–11
  • Cron job for availability reminder emails
  • Reminder frequency settings in foster profile dashboard
  • Mobile responsive design review across all pages
  • Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.1 AA)
  • Error states, empty states, and loading skeletons
5
Testing & Launch
Week 12
  • End-to-end tests: Playwright or Cypress for critical flows
  • Unit tests for API routes and matching logic
  • Staging environment QA with real test data
  • Production deploy to Vercel + Railway
  • Analytics setup: PostHog or Plausible
